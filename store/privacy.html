Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-09

This policy explains what happens to personal data when you visit this store or buy a licence. The seller is Itikia Digital Solutions, Nairobi, Kenya. Contact for anything on this page: hello@itikia.co.ke.

The short version. This site is a static page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no trackers. Payment details never touch our servers — the payment processor handles them. We only hold what you email us.

1. What this site itself collects

Nothing that identifies you. The pages are static files; they do not set cookies, do not use local storage for tracking, and do not include advertising or analytics scripts. The demo on the front page processes a file you choose inside your own browser — that file is not uploaded anywhere.

2. What the hosting provider processes

Like any website, the host (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data to serve pages and defend against abuse — IP address, requested URL, user agent and timestamps — under its own security and retention practices. We do not build profiles from it.

3. What happens when you buy

Payments are processed by Paddle, which acts as merchant of record. Paddle collects your name, email, billing address and payment method, issues the invoice, handles refunds and meets tax obligations. That data is processed by Paddle under Paddle’s privacy policy — we do not receive or store your card details.

We receive the information needed to fulfil your order: the order reference, the product and price, the buyer name and email, and the country. We use it to deliver the licence, to support you afterwards, and to keep the accounting records we are legally required to keep.

4. Email

If you write to hello@itikia.co.ke, we keep your message and address for as long as needed to answer and to support the licence you hold. Email is routed and stored by our mail provider (Cloudflare Email Routing forwards to a mailbox we control). We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not sell or share your address.

5. Why we are allowed to process it

We process order data to perform our contract with you (delivering the licence), to comply with legal obligations (tax and accounting records), and on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering support email and preventing abuse.

6. How long we keep it

Order and licence records are kept for as long as the licence relationship lasts and then for the period required by tax and accounting law. Support email is kept while it is useful for your support history, and deleted on request where we are not required to retain it.

7. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 — and under equivalent laws where you live — you may ask to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, object to processing, or ask for a copy. Write to hello@itikia.co.ke. For data held by Paddle as merchant of record (billing details, payment method, tax records), contact Paddle directly; we will help you route the request.

8. Sharing

We share personal data only with the processors needed to run this business: the payment processor (Paddle), the hosting and email providers, and — where strictly required — tax authorities. We do not sell personal data.

9. Children

This is a developer tool. The store is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top. The version published when you buy applies to that purchase.

11. Contact
